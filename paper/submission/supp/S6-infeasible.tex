\section{The full ledger of cells that produced no valid row}
\label{supp:infeasible}

This section is the complete accounting of primary-grid cells that produced no
valid row, and of why each one produced none. It supports four statements in the
manuscript. First, the quality-gate verdict of Section~6: of the 6{,}480, 210 and
136 error rows in the stable, shift and placebo regimes, exactly zero, zero and
zero are machine failures, and by class (structural / method / residual) the
counts are 3{,}376 / 1{,}106 / 1{,}998 in the stable control, 126 / 56 / 28 under
the break and 0 / 98 / 38 in the placebo. Second, the design-floor paragraph of
Section~4, which promises that the cells lost to a family--mechanism pair's
minimum training length are tabulated cell by cell, with the longer
stable-regime list, here. Third, the selection caveats of Section~8: the sparse
VAR is thinned by its own registered explosive-draw rejection rule rather than by
an admissibility floor, and the stable control is scored on eighteen populations
because HRV and KOR enter HMD too late to meet the registered floor of fifteen
contiguous training years at any of the thirteen stable origins. Fourth, the
statement in Section~9 that those exclusions are registered-rule admissibility
outcomes rather than discretionary omissions. Every error row counted here is
excluded from every reported mean and is counted in the denominator note of the
table it would otherwise have entered; no mean in this study silently omits one.

\paragraph{The classes.} The gate is \texttt{scripts/final\_qa.py} and it assigns
each cell that produced no valid row to one class. A \emph{structural} cell is a
design-floor cell: the panel is shorter than the mechanism's calibration or
ensemble requirement, or shorter than the registered admissibility floor of
$n_{\text{train}}\ge 15$ complete contiguous training years (Addendum~3, \S4 and
\S11). It is a property of the design, tabulated and dropped by the common-cell
restriction. A \emph{method} cell is one in which the family's own sampler
declined to produce a predictive law under a registered rule -- the sparse VAR's
rejection of explosive coefficient draws (Addendum~3, \S7: rejection, never
clipping) or the Poisson-composition overflow guard -- and is therefore a finding
about the family rather than an accident. A \emph{machine} cell is a broken run;
it aborts table generation, so this class must be empty for any table in this
paper to exist, and it is empty in all three regimes. The residual class, printed
as \emph{other} in the ledgers below, collects family-specific admissibility
failures that are neither of the first two: all 28 shift rows are the
multi-output Gaussian process's trailing-block floor of forty years, and the
1{,}998 stable rows are origins preceding a population's first observed year
(1{,}416), that same GP floor (574) and LUX under the banded VAR's
regression-row minimum (8).

\paragraph{What the two ledgers show.} Table~\ref{tab:infeasible} is the summary:
one row per regime, family, mechanism and class, carrying the HMD codes of the
populations affected, their number, and the number of error rows pooled over
populations, sexes and origins. Table~\ref{tab:infeasible-full} is the
unabridged version: one row per affected population--family--mechanism cell,
carrying the class, the recorded reason string rather than an excerpt of a
grouped reason, and the number of rows affected. It is what the split of the
stable method class into 1{,}027 rejected sparse VAR draws and 78 Poisson
composition overflows is read from, and what shows the overflow guard firing 79,
7 and 6 times across the three regimes, always on the longest exposure panels;
1{,}105 of the 1{,}106 stable method rows are the sparse VAR, the remaining row a
CNN-LC overflow in ISL, and the placebo method class is 96 sparse VAR rows plus
2 CNN-LC rows. Both are generated by \texttt{scripts/make\_tables.py} from the
same quality-gate output, and are typeset as \texttt{longtable}s so that they may
run over several pages; nothing in either is typed by hand. Each states its
regime, population count and total number of error rows in its own note, and the
multi-output GP rows in both come from the second-pass parquet files
(\texttt{results/<regime>\_gp.parquet}) merged by regime.

Both fragments are reproduced exactly as generated for the full study, so their
notes describe that document's layout: where a note calls the summary ledger
the one in ``the main text'' and the per-cell listing an ``appendix'' table, both
are in fact Tables~\ref{tab:infeasible} and~\ref{tab:infeasible-full} of this
supplement, and the manuscript cites them by those numbers.
