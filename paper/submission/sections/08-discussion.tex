\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{The break sorts the families; it does not create the miscalibration}
\label{sec:discussion-standing}

Most of the interval failure predates any break; the break re-orders the
families by how far they already stood from nominal, and on which side. The
stable control, with no structural event in its test years, already puts the
full-age classical cores below nominal at every level---0.324--0.365 at 0.50,
0.522--0.566 at 0.80, 0.682--0.731 at 0.95 (Section~\ref{sec:results-stable};
Table~S3). SVD Lee--Carter's 0.692 in 2020--2024 would be laid to the pandemic;
0.702 of it was there in 1990--2014.

\Htwo\ therefore fails in its registered one-sided form while the two-sided
restatement of Addendum~3~\S8 holds in 27 of the 50 cells: a fall is
degradation only for an arm that began below nominal. Table~S3 sorts the arms
four ways---standing failures barely moved or deepened; near-nominal arms
damaged, the sparse VAR's split-conformal band going from a registered
departure of 0.002 to 0.910 coverage; CBD's departure growing to the grid's
largest, from an unsound start; and over-wide arms pulled towards nominal.

\Hthree\ returns no registered verdict: Addendum~3 re-specified the comparator
as each cell's own simulated-path-implied joint coverage, which is not
computable from the columns the runner emitted
(Section~\ref{sec:results-joint}), so the marginal-to-joint gap is a
measurement, not a test. Positive path dependence does not rescue the level.
The four control arms at or above 0.95 jointly (the NB head 0.956 and 0.953,
the sparse VAR 0.951 and 0.985) get there only by being over-wide at the single
level, and none does at the break; the copula band leads eight of ten family
blocks in the control and nine of ten at the break yet is beaten inside its own
family by the NB head's dropout arm, 0.956 to 0.922 and 0.897 to 0.868
(Table~S4).

\emph{A calibration audit must include a quiet control period.} A shift-only
audit cannot separate a model that broke from one never calibrated, nor either
from one over-wide that got lucky: at the break the sparse VAR's native law
looks the grid's best-calibrated native arm, a rank it earns only by having
been over-wide beforehand by roughly what the shock consumed.

\subsection{What the family-level split is evidence of}
\label{sec:discussion-split}

Section~\ref{sec:results} refutes the neural-versus-classical conjecture in its
strong form: the arms near nominal in both regimes carry their uncertainty in a
distributional head or in integration over the model's own parameters, the rest
in the process risk of an extrapolated period index. That is association, not
mechanism---the grid crossed family with mechanism, not index extrapolation
with distributional head. Nor are the calibrated arms merely vaguer: in the
control the two smallest 95\% interval scores among full-age distributional
arms belong to the two best-covering native laws ($\IS_{95}$ 1.455 for the NB
head, 1.474 for the sparse VAR) against 2.330 for SVD Lee--Carter and 3.056 for
Poisson Lee--Carter, and neural-LC dropout, the one arm visibly buying coverage
with width, is caught by the width penalty
(Section~\ref{sec:results-contrasts}). CBD is excluded, its 55--99 support not
being the full-age families'.

\subsection{The age profile is standing, and it settles a disagreement with the prior literature}
\label{sec:discussion-age}

\Hfour's old-age concentration is family-specific
(Section~\ref{sec:results-age}) and, where it holds, a property of the quiet
control: the stable age gradients of SVD Lee--Carter and of the LSTM ensemble
barely move at the break (Table~\ref{tab:h4-age-main}; Table~S5)---a standing
feature of index extrapolation, whose loadings are largest where the death
counts are. APC/RH is the counter-case of a genuinely break-induced gradient,
0.751, 0.729 and 0.720 across ages 0--24, 25--64 and 65--99 in the control
against 0.732, 0.661 and 0.589 at the break; neural-LC dropout's collapse at
young ages is standing too, 0.529 against 0.538.

That settles a disagreement in the published record, on a wider age range and
more origins and populations than the two-age comparison
\citet{dowd2010backtesting} rested their contrary finding on; what survives
from it, that the ordering is not universal, our evidence makes more strongly.
The direction reverses at the break in 34 of the 50 cells, 26 of them the 30
conformal arms (SVD Lee--Carter's EnbPI arm covers 0.884 at 25--64 against
0.938 at 65--99), the rest every arm of CNN-LC, Poisson Lee--Carter, the GP and
CBD---which is fitted on ages 55--99, so its ``25--64'' column is a 55--64 stub
and is not comparable with the full-age families'. The NB head and the sparse
VAR preserve it in four of their five arms each, by margins of 0.044 and below.
Old-age concentration is thus a property of index extrapolation, not of
mortality forecasting at large, and a conformal wrapper does not merely flatten
it: on 26 of 30 wrapped arms it tips it the other way.

\subsection{Derived quantities carry the least reliable intervals in the study, and already do so in quiet years}
\label{sec:discussion-derived}

In every full-age family the intervals on $e_{65}$ and $\annuity$ are worse
calibrated than the same arm's rate-level intervals, and for the index
extrapolators the deterioration is already near full size in the quiet control
(Table~\ref{tab:h5-actuarial-main}; Table~S6). Integration concentrates the
failure rather than averaging it away, answering \citet{cairns2011mortality}'s
attenuation alternative in ordinary valuation practice and not only in a
crisis.

Two qualifications. CBD is the exception, integration not hurting it on its
truncated 55--99 table. And for arms well calibrated on rates the failure is a
break effect, not a standing one: from rates to $\annuity$ the sparse VAR's
native law loses 0.008 in the control against 0.351 at the break, the GP 0.082
against 0.311, the NB head's ensemble 0.174 against 0.506. The sorting logic
above recurs---badly under-covering arms cover \emph{better} on the derived
quantities at the break while arms calibrated in quiet years collapse (the GP
0.863 to 0.594), CBD again excepted---and that first movement is not a repair:
SVD Lee--Carter under-states $e_{65}$ systematically, by a mean 0.84 years in
the control and 0.57 at the break, so coverage rose because a standing
under-projection of longevity was partly cancelled by a mortality shock. Those
two means rest on different support (limitations below), so the narrowing
describes two columns, not a period effect; a reserving basis that appears to
improve under a pandemic for that reason has not been validated by it.

\subsection{Reading the conformal attribution}
\label{sec:discussion-conformal}

The Diebold--Mariano block (Table~S9) is the crossed design's attribution
finding: \emph{wrap what is broken; do not wrap what is calibrated}. Replacing
a model's own uncertainty with a residual-quantile geometry is signed---better
on the 95\% interval score for the four classical cores whose native law is
wrong, worse for the three whose native law is sound, all seven significant at
the break and five already at the same sign in the quiet control. Which side a
family falls on is a property of the family--mechanism pair, not of the wrapper
or the regime, so the wrapper is insurance in ordinary years, not only across
breaks.

Four cautions. The pattern does not reproduce at the second break: in the
placebo all six families admitting the contrast favour the native law, only the
NB head's significantly ($-0.461$), CBD's reversal marginal ($-0.476$,
$p=0.071$), the rest indistinguishable from zero, over nine horizons on eleven
populations with pre-1914 calibration residuals. The repair is empirical, not
guaranteed: under an exchangeability violated by design (limitations below) the
wrappers still held 95\% coverage of 0.848--0.981, which
\citet{barber2023conformal}'s bounds permit but do not promise, and whoever
adopts a wrapper for the next break inherits the asterisk. The wrapper
equalises the marginal level and nothing else: split and EnbPI bands that cover
marginally still lose the path, while the copula band that holds the path is
eliminated from the interval-score confidence sets in five families at the
break and two in the control, so choosing among wrappers is choosing which
quantity is calibrated. And a conformal cell has no distributional paths, so it
contributes nothing to the derived-quantity audit (Section~S2); what a band on
log rates should mean for a functional across ages was not registered.

\subsection{Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion-limitations}
\label{sec:discussion-limits}

All but the last three were committed before any regime result was read, so
that none could be selected afterwards to excuse or inflate a finding; the last
three arose during the sweeps and the analysis, and are dated.

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{The effective sample size is one.} Every shift-regime number
  describes one pandemic in twenty places, sixteen for the GP. Clustering and
  imposing the null make the standard errors honest \emph{given the event}, not
  the event a sample of events. The audit answers whether these intervals
  survived this break, whether they were calibrated before it and, through the
  placebo, whether the pattern resembles the previous one---not whether they
  survive the next.
\item \emph{The grid is ragged, so claims are contrasts.} Seventy cells on
  paper, fifty in fact; main effects would confound the factor with
  admissibility, so every claim is a contrast within a stated sub-grid, never
  averaged across the classical and neural halves. The 27-of-50 and 34-of-50
  counts are descriptive tallies carrying no inference.
\item \emph{Conformal cells carry secondary proper scores.} A band is not a
  distribution: their CRPS, log score and PIT come from uniform draws inside it
  and are flagged and unranked (Sections~S2 and~S5), so such a cell's CRPS
  beside a native cell's compares a convention with a forecast; coverage, width
  and interval score are unaffected. Conformal predictive systems would remove
  this.
\item \emph{2024 may be revised.} HMD flags no provisional years and re-issues
  whole series each release; the shift regime's $h=5$ rests on 2024, and two of
  its twenty populations (USA, CHL) carry census-based exposures documented as
  subject to revision. The pre-registered robustness run drops 2024; Addendum~1
  adds runs dropping USA and CHL and contrasting register- with census-based
  populations. Where $h=5$ and $h\le4$ disagree, trust $h\le4$; a later vintage
  would be re-run and any change in headline coverage reported as a
  vintage-revision effect.
\item \emph{SVD Lee--Carter is over-dispersed by construction.} Gate~1 found
  its intervals wider than nominal on data simulated from a Poisson Lee--Carter
  process, the SVD estimate of $\kappa_t$ absorbing observation noise that
  inflates the random-walk innovation variance---as
  \citet{girosi2007understanding} would predict, not a harness artefact. An
  SVD-LC row that looks better calibrated than Poisson-LC may be so partly by
  being vaguer; the interval score and the Murphy resolution term (Table~S8),
  not coverage, separate them.
\item \emph{Exchangeability is violated by design.} Conformal calibration
  residuals come from pre-cutoff years and the test years are precisely those
  chosen to differ from them; \citet{barber2023conformal} bound the coverage
  gap and we measure it rather than assume it away. The conformal arms are
  audited, not endorsed.
\item \emph{Neural families are budget-limited.} Ensembles of ten across three
  regimes fix the search to the small pre-specified grids of Section~S2, tuned
  on a five-year inner time split. Better tuning might improve point accuracy;
  whether it would improve calibration is not known, and the audited fragility
  of the year-feature extrapolation is a property of the specification, not a
  tuning accident.
\item \emph{Data quality in the placebo window.} Military deaths are
  reconstructed in the belligerent series and excluded in the civilian-only
  one; placebo results are descriptive, reported pooled and by the
  pre-specified strata (Table~S10), the neutral register-based stratum carrying
  the 1918-versus-2020 comparison.
\item \emph{Three further pre-committed items.} Ages 0--99 modelled singly
  leave old-age cells noisy in small populations, and the age-cap-90 run tests
  whether \Hfour\ is an artefact of that noise; the log-score convention rounds
  fractional deaths, with a CRPS-on-counts sensitivity; and one evaluation
  module by one author is a strength for consistency and a weakness for
  independent verification, checked by the synthetic-truth gate and the
  \pkg{StMoMo} oracle, with the code public.
\item \emph{The GP rows audit a sixty-year-window variant on a reduced
  population set} (Addendum~4, registered 2026-08-29 during the sweeps, before
  any GP cell on a longer panel had produced a result). Memory made the exact
  multitask GP infeasible on the long panels, so the family trains on the
  trailing sixty complete years under every mechanism
  (Section~\ref{sec:design-deviations}), and every GP number here describes
  that variant, not the registered full-panel specification. Its minimum
  training-block rule also removes populations by regime---sixteen stable and
  sixteen shift against eighteen and twenty for the other nine families, eight
  of the placebo's eleven---so every GP comparison stays inside its own
  sub-grid.
\item \emph{The stable control is scored on eighteen populations, not twenty}
  (recorded 2026-09-02 at the stable QA gate). HRV and KOR miss the registered
  floor of fifteen contiguous training years at all thirteen stable origins, so
  each stable column is a mean over eighteen populations, sixteen for the GP.
  The sparse VAR's explosive-draw rejection rule thins it further: 297 of the
  520 (population, sex, origin) units survive natively, and of the 223
  excluded, 145 are method-class rejections against 50 structural and 28 other
  (Table~S11). Stable-versus-shift comparisons therefore do not rest on one
  population set; every contrast reported with a test statistic is computed on
  the intersection of cells in which both arms produced a valid row
  (Addendum~3~\S11), the cluster counts of Table~S9 stating it.
\item \emph{The 99.5\% longevity tail was not emitted by the swept passes}
  (recorded 2026-08-30 at analysis; closed as future work 2026-09-04). The
  runner persists the 2.5\% and 97.5\% sample quantiles of the derived
  quantities, not the 99.5\%, so the frequency of a nominally once-in-200-year
  $\annuity$ exceedance cannot be evaluated from the results files, and no
  tail-frequency claim is made anywhere in this paper; it was descriptive and
  not registered. Per-cell seeds are recorded, so a re-score with a persisted
  99.5\% column would reproduce the original predictive samples exactly; that
  is left to future work, and the 97.5\% breach frequencies of
  Section~\ref{sec:actuarial} are what this study reports at the top of the
  distribution.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{What the stable and placebo regimes settle, and what they cannot}
\label{sec:discussion-regimes}

Both registered comparative clauses depend on the stable regime---\Htwo's
growth in $|\text{coverage}-\text{nominal}|$ per family and mechanism, and
\Hfive's excess of the shift departure on $e_{65}$ and $\annuity$ over the
stable one, per arm---and both return a split verdict rather than the
registered universal. The control delivers discriminating power, not novelty;
it cannot convert an event study into a sample of events. The placebo,
descriptive by registration (Table~S10), repeats \Hthree's horizon
dependence---joint coverage beats independence at each arm's own marginal rate
in 45 of the 46 admissible cells even over nine horizons---but at a far lower
level, below the nominal $0.95^9=0.630$ in 37 of those 46 and at 0.335 for SVD
Lee--Carter's native law (Table~S4), with the conformal sign pattern not
recurring at all. It supports no transfer regression and no formal test of
COVID-specificity; its eleven populations overlap only partly with the shift
regime's twenty; the sparse VAR's rejection rule leaves that family one
admissible mechanism there; the pre-specified strata carry the data-quality
reading; and its first test year, 1914, precedes the influenza wave, so its
$h=1$ column is a pre-shock year in a way the shift regime's is not. Two events
leave the effective-sample-size limitation where it is.

\subsection{What this paper does not do}

It proposes no model, no repair and no recommendation about which family to
use; the conformal contrast is reported with its cost and its placebo failure
beside it, not as a method contribution. The one recommendation---that a
calibration audit include a quiet control period---is about measurement, and
follows from this study having had a different and wrong headline without one.
Explainability of the neural families is deferred to a companion paper that
reuses these fits.
