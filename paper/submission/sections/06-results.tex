\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

\noindent\textit{Every table and figure below is generated from the per-cell
results files; no number in them is typed by hand, and every figure quoted in the
prose is transcribed from them.}

The three registered regimes of Table~\ref{tab:regimes} were swept on the same
grid, through the same predictive-sample interface and the same evaluation code
path, each read only after its post-sweep QA gate passed. The stable sweep
produced 26{,}000 cell rows of which 6{,}480 are error rows (24.9\%), the shift
sweep 2{,}000 of which 210 (10.5\%), the placebo sweep 1{,}100 of which 136
(12.4\%); every error row is classified below and excluded from every mean in
this paper.

The sentence that organises the rest of this section is that the stable control
is not a quiet backdrop against which the break stands out. Most of the
miscalibration measured here is already present with nothing broken, and the
pandemic mainly re-orders the grid rather than degrading it uniformly.

\subsection{Validation gates, and what was scored}
\label{sec:results-infeasible}

The three pre-sweep gates passed before any real-data result was read; gate~1's
one substantive finding---mild over-dispersion of two-stage SVD Lee--Carter on
Poisson data---is carried into the SVD-LC rows below. A fourth, post-sweep gate
classifies every cell that produced no valid row before any table is generated.
It passed on all three regimes and the machine-failure class is empty in each: of
the 6{,}480, 210 and 136 error rows, exactly 0, 0 and 0 are machine failures. By
class (structural / method / residual) the counts are 126 / 56 / 28 under the
break, 3{,}376 / 1{,}106 / 1{,}998 in the control and 0 / 98 / 38 in the placebo;
the ledger is Table~S11, the per-cell reasons Table~S12.

\emph{Structural} cells are design-floor cells: the panel is shorter than a
mechanism's calibration or ensemble requirement, or under the registered
admissibility floor of $n_{\text{train}}\ge 15$ complete training years
(Addendum~3, \S4). They follow the training lengths of
Table~\ref{tab:populations} exactly, and the stable share is largest because a
floor binding at one origin binds at every earlier one; the placebo has none, its
populations having continuous 1908--1922 coverage. \emph{Method} cells are those in
which a family's own sampler declines to produce a predictive law under a
registered rule---the sparse VAR's explosive-coefficient-draw rejection
(Addendum~3, \S7: rejection, never clipping) or the Poisson-composition overflow
guard. That class is the sparse VAR alone under the break; 1{,}105 of the
1{,}106 stable method rows (1{,}027 rejected draws, 78 overflow, split by reason
in Table~S12), the remaining row a CNN-LC overflow in ISL; and 96 sparse VAR rows
plus 2 CNN-LC rows in the placebo. The overflow guard fires 79, 7 and 6 times,
always on the longest exposure panels. Structural cells are thus a property of
the design, and method cells a property of the family under its own registered
rule; neither is a machine failure.

The residual class collects family-specific admissibility failures: all 28 shift
rows are the multi-output GP's trailing-block floor; the stable 1{,}998 are
origins preceding a population's first observed year (1{,}416), that GP floor
(574) and LUX under the banded VAR's regression-row minimum (8). The GP floor
changes what every GP row of this paper means. The family needs forty training
years, and that floor, not the fifteen-year rule, removes CHL, HKG, HRV and KOR
from every GP cell; the sixty-year training-window cap of Addendum~4 is a
separate deviation. The GP is read on sixteen populations against twenty for
the other nine families under the break, sixteen against eighteen in the control,
and no contrast crosses that boundary: each is computed on the cells where every
compared arm produced a valid row, with the intersection size reported
(Addendum~3, \S11).

\subsection{Calibration where nothing breaks: the stable control}
\label{sec:results-stable}

Over 13 expanding origins from 1990 to 2014, on panels with no structural break
in them and with the horizons, grid and code path of the shift regime, the
classical intervals already cover far below nominal: under their own predictive
law SVD Lee--Carter 0.702 at the nominal 95\% level, Poisson Lee--Carter 0.682,
APC/RH 0.731, CBD 0.901 on its 45-age support. Nor is the shortfall an artefact
of the tail level: at nominal 0.50 and 0.80 SVD Lee--Carter covers 0.365 and
0.562, Poisson Lee--Carter 0.324 and 0.522. The point-forecast neural mechanisms
are worse still, and the arms carrying a count likelihood or a full posterior do
not under-cover in the quiet regime at all (NB head 0.975 natively and 0.988
under the deep ensemble, sparse VAR 0.988, CNN-LC dropout 0.954, GP 0.945); that
group over-covers, and the slack is what the break later consumes.

The other registered failures are present in the control too: joint path coverage
below marginal in all fifty cells with 21 of them under 0.774
(Section~\ref{sec:results-joint}), and Lee--Carter's age gradient already at
0.888 / 0.760 / 0.501 (Section~\ref{sec:results-age}). The derived quantities of
\Hfive---what an actuary actually quotes---are worse here than any rate-level
figure: SVD Lee--Carter natively covers 0.253 for $\annuity$ at 2\% and 0.256 for
$e_{65}$, the LSTM ensemble 0.157 for $\annuity$, against a nominal 0.95
(Section~\ref{sec:actuarial}). The propagation is not universal (sparse VAR
native 0.980 and 0.983, CBD bootstrap 0.934 for $\annuity$), but for the
index-extrapolating families an annuity factor computed from these models carries
essentially no reliable interval even when nothing breaks. The shift regime is
therefore read not against the nominal level, which most of these arms never
attained, but against this control.

\subsection{H1: ranking by RMSE versus ranking by proper scores}

\Hone\ ranks the distributional arms three times per regime---by mean $\RMSE$ on
log rates, mean $\CRPS$ and mean Poisson log score (Table~S2)---on the block's
common cells (22 of 40 units under the break). Conformal arms are excluded, their
$\CRPS$, log score and PIT being placeholders from uniform-in-interval samples, and
CBD is ranked separately, a mean over its 45 ages not being a mean over 100.

\Hone\ as registered---the RMSE and CRPS orderings disagree, rank correlation
below one, at least one top-three inversion---is supported in that mild form and
far more strongly against the log score. Spearman
$\rho(\text{RMSE},\text{CRPS}) = 0.95$, with the registered inversion present:
the RMSE top three (LSTM-$\kappa_t$ dropout and ensemble, then SVD-LC native)
share one member with the CRPS top three (the two sparse VAR arms, then LSTM
dropout). Against the log score the ordering nearly dissolves, $\rho=0.12$: the
LSTM ensemble is 2nd by RMSE and 16th of 18 by log score, SVD-LC native 3rd and
12th, the NB head mid-pack by RMSE (ranks 10--12) but holding the top three
log-score places with CNN-LC dropout, and neural-LC dropout last by RMSE and 5th
by log score. A cluster bootstrap of 2{,}000 replicates over the thirteen
populations surviving the common-cell restriction gives $[0.77, 0.96]$ and
$[-0.16, 0.21]$: the first excludes one, so the below-one clause does not depend
on which populations entered the block; the second contains zero, so at this
resolution the log-score ordering is indistinguishable from one unrelated to the
RMSE ordering. This is a property of the metrics, not of the break---$0.81$ and
$-0.18$ in the control on the 217 of 520 units kept, $0.85$ and $0.16$ in the
placebo on 9 of 22---so \Hone\ is retained as the validity check it was
registered to be.

\subsection{H2: marginal coverage across the break}

\inputtable{tab-h2-coverage-main}

Table~\ref{tab:h2-coverage-main} gives mean coverage at 50, 80 and 95\% and the mean
95\% interval score for the arms carrying their own predictive law; the full
50-cell version, conformal arms included, is Table~S3.

\Htwo\ has a registered one-sided form and a two-sided restatement, and the
verdicts differ. \textbf{The one-sided form---nominal 95\% intervals under-cover
in the shift regime \emph{for every model family}---is not supported.} The
classical cores under their own law do under-cover severely (Poisson Lee--Carter
0.624, APC/RH 0.654, SVD-LC 0.692, CBD 0.799 on its 45-age support), but the NB
head covers 0.933 natively, 0.956 under the deep ensemble and 0.972 under
dropout, CNN-LC dropout 0.962, the GP 0.905 and the sparse VAR 0.937--0.938 on
the cells its rejection rule admits. Several arms sit at or above nominal through
the break, so the miscalibration is not even uniformly downward.

The two-sided quantity registered in Addendum~3 (\S8), $|\text{coverage}-0.95|$,
hypothesised to grow from control to shift, gives the sharper verdict: it grows
in 27 of the 50 admissible cells, a bare majority rather than a general
degradation. Raw coverage falls in 34 of 50, the number a paper without a control would have
reported as damage; but a fall is a degradation only for a cell that began below
nominal, and 16 of the 50 began above it. The break sorts
the grid by which side of nominal each arm started on. Of the sixteen
over-covering arms, eleven move \emph{towards} nominal (sparse VAR native 0.988
to 0.937 and bootstrap 0.996 to 0.938; NB head 0.988 to 0.956 as deep ensemble,
0.988 to 0.972 under dropout, 0.975 to 0.933 natively), the pandemic consuming
slack their own law had built in. Of the thirty-four at or below nominal,
twenty-two move further away (Poisson Lee--Carter 0.682 to 0.624, APC/RH 0.731 to
0.654, CBD 0.901 to 0.799, SVD Lee--Carter 0.702 to 0.692). The honest statement
is therefore not that COVID broke these intervals: Poisson Lee--Carter's native
band missed 0.318 of observations before the pandemic existed and 0.376 during
it, the pandemic contributing the last six percentage points of a
thirty-three-point shortfall.

The registration declined a directional claim on neural-versus-classical
ordering, and the conjecture that motivated the audit---degradation larger for
the neural families---is refuted in its strong form in both regimes.
LSTM-$\kappa_t$ (0.638 ensemble and 0.667 dropout in the control, 0.647 and 0.685
under the break) and the neural-LC ensemble (0.758 and 0.694) break exactly like
the classical cores, while sibling neural arms with a count likelihood or dropout
smoothing are the best-covered arms in the study in every regime. What separates
broken arms from calibrated ones is the family--mechanism pair, not the presence
of a neural network (Section~\ref{sec:results-contrasts}).

The interval score orders the same failure the same way in both regimes---Poisson
Lee--Carter 3.056 in the control and 4.456 under the break, APC/RH 2.398 and
3.401, against NB native 1.455 and 1.684---so the broken natives pay twice, in
missed observations and in width; the largest score is neural-LC dropout, 5.559
under the break at coverage 0.811, an arm buying coverage with width. A conformal
band moves any family's 95\% coverage into a narrow range however broken the
native law is: 0.890--0.948 for split and EnbPI and 0.951--0.985 for the copula
arm in the control, 0.848--0.944 and 0.939--0.981 under the break (Table~S3).

\subsection{H3: joint path coverage}
\label{sec:results-joint}

\inputtable{tab-h3-joint-main}

Table~\ref{tab:h3-joint-main} sets mean marginal 95\% coverage beside mean joint path
coverage: the coverage a mortality paper reports beside the coverage a liability
depends on (full version Table~S4; Figure~S8). Joint coverage lies below marginal
in 100 of 100 (regime, cell) pairs, all fifty stable and all fifty shift arms,
with a mean gap of 0.127 in the control and 0.168 under the break. That count is descriptive and largely arithmetic, and
reported as such: joint coverage is bounded above by the smallest marginal
coverage on the path, and at nominal 0.95 over five horizons independence alone
gives $0.95^5 = 0.774$, a gap of 0.176 \emph{larger} than either observed mean.

The informative benchmark is independence \emph{at each arm's own marginal rate}:
an arm covering $c$ marginally would hold $c^{H}$ of its paths were its horizons
independent. Measured so, almost every arm beats independence---50 of 50 stable
and 50 of 50 shift cells, by $+0.007$ to $+0.344$ in the control and $+0.014$ to
$+0.328$ under the break, and 45 of the 46 placebo cells over nine horizons---the
one exception being the sparse VAR's Poisson-bootstrap placebo arm, 0.748 joint
against a benchmark of 0.756 (computed from unrounded marginal coverage; the
printed 0.969 would give 0.753). Mortality forecast errors are strongly
positively dependent down the path, the same drift error persisting across every
horizon: the dependence \citet{dowd2010backtesting} named in a footnote and did
not measure, and measuring it across ten families, seven mechanisms and three
regimes is \Hthree's contribution. It also means the marginal-to-joint gap is not
evidence of miscalibration---most of it is arithmetic, and the dependence works
in the forecaster's favour.

What is supportable is the level of joint coverage, not the size of the gap.
Joint path coverage falls below 0.774 in 21 of the 50 stable cells and 30 of the
50 shift cells---arms missing the whole five-year path more often than a
genuinely nominal pointwise construction would with no horizon dependence helping
it. For the broken classical natives the collapse is near-total in both
regimes (Poisson Lee--Carter 0.682 marginal to 0.422 joint in the control, 0.624
to 0.298 under the break), but good marginal calibration does not remove the gap:
the sparse VAR falls natively from 0.988 to 0.951 and from 0.937 to 0.819, and
the NB head's native law under the break sets 0.933 marginal against 0.795
joint---one trajectory in five missed by an arm whose reported marginal coverage
is nominal. The one mechanism constructed to be joint over $h=1,\dots,H$ behaves
as constructed in both regimes: the copula-conformal arm's gaps run $-0.043$ to
$-0.064$ in the control and $-0.049$ to $-0.136$ under the break, the smallest in
its family block everywhere but the NB head.

The registered comparator itself is not available, and the verdict must be stated
with that absence in it. Addendum~3 (\S8) re-specified the comparator as each
cell's own simulated-path-implied joint coverage, so that the realised-minus-implied
gap could take either sign; the runner's column contract carries realised joint
coverage but does not emit the implied quantity, which is therefore \emph{not
computable} from the columns the runner wrote. The registered paired test is not
run and \Hthree\ is not adjudicated in its registered form. In its place stand
the marginal-to-joint gap, descriptive and near-tautological in sign, and the
independence comparison, which is neither. The implied comparator is recomputable from the recorded per-row
seeds and would split the residual gap into horizon dependence and
miscalibration; it is declared unfinished business, not an omission discovered
after the fact.

\subsection{H4: where in age the intervals fail}
\label{sec:results-age}

\inputtable{tab-h4-age-main}

\Hfour\ as registered---shift-regime coverage at ages 65--99 lower than at 25--64
\emph{for every family}---is not supported as a universal; the age profile is
family-specific (Table~\ref{tab:h4-age-main}; all 50 cells in all three regimes in
Table~S5, Figures~S4--S6). It is supported, sharply, for the full-age families
whose extrapolated period index \emph{also} broke in
Table~\ref{tab:h2-coverage-main}: across ages 0--24, 25--64 and 65--99 under the break
SVD Lee--Carter falls 0.882 / 0.725 / 0.518, APC/RH 0.732 / 0.661 / 0.589 and the
LSTM ensemble most steeply of all 0.893 / 0.687 / 0.426; an interval sold as 95\%
contains the realised old-age rate in about half those cells.

The control settles what the break contributed: the gradient is already there
with nothing broken (SVD Lee--Carter 0.888 / 0.760 / 0.501, LSTM ensemble
0.858 / 0.700 / 0.409), a standing property of index extrapolation rather than a
pandemic effect, and what 2020--2024 adds is small and in the wrong direction,
old-age coverage moving 0.501 to 0.518 and 0.409 to 0.426, both \emph{towards}
nominal, a band already missing half its old-age outcomes having little room left
to lose. This is the one hypothesis whose registered direction the prior
literature disputed: \citet{dowd2010backtesting}, on six classical models and one
population, found the higher age behaving better. Across three regimes, ten
families and up to twenty populations we find the reverse for the full-age
index-extrapolating cores, in quiet years as well as across the break.

The reversal is nonetheless common: under the break, coverage at 65--99 exceeds
coverage at 25--64 in 34 of the 50 cells---26 of the 30 conformal arms, whose
Mondrian strata are calibrated on these bands separately and therefore flatten or
invert the profile by construction, and 8 of the 20 arms carrying a family's own
uncertainty (both CBD arms, both CNN-LC arms, the GP, neural-LC dropout and both
Poisson Lee--Carter arms, per-arm values in Table~S5). Five of those eight
reverse by five hundredths or less, which is no age
profile in either direction: Poisson Lee--Carter is uniformly poor across all
three bands under the break (0.639 / 0.602 / 0.639) and in the control
(0.721 / 0.664 / 0.676), and the GP is flat and near-nominal
(0.908 / 0.900 / 0.909).

CBD is excluded from the supported class for a different reason, and that caveat
governs every CBD column in this paper: it is fitted on ages 55--99, so its
``25--64'' column is a 55--64 stub, not comparable with the full-age families'
bands. On the stated criterion it would belong there---it extrapolates two period
indices by a bivariate random walk with drift and its native interval broke,
0.901 to 0.799---but its reversal is not a break effect either, being already
present in the control (0.913 against 0.861 natively, 0.930 against 0.888 under
the bootstrap). The neural-LC ensemble is the exception in the other direction,
0.619 at ages 0--24 against 0.793 and 0.633 above, its failure concentrated at
the young ages, where exposure is largest and deaths fewest.

\subsection{PIT uniformity, reliability and the Murphy decomposition}

Table~S7 gives the mean KS distance of the randomised PIT from the uniform and
the share of cells rejected at a nominal $0.05$; that $p$-value is descriptive
only, PIT values within a cell being dependent across ages and horizons, and the
shape of each departure is read from Figures~S10--S12. Under the break the
ordering matches Table~\ref{tab:h2-coverage-main} (smallest distances GP 0.172, NB
head 0.182--0.189, sparse VAR 0.188; largest Poisson Lee--Carter 0.374 and APC/RH
0.325), but the levels carry the sharper message: no arm is distributionally
calibrated, with or without a break. The calibrated end is the same in the
control while the poorly calibrated end re-orders, the LSTM arms holding the two
largest distances there (0.335 and 0.322), so a PIT ranking read off one regime
does not transfer to the other. The rejected share never drops beneath 0.905 in
the control or 0.844 under the break: the NB head's 95\% interval can be
essentially nominal while its predictive law, read over all levels at once, is
rejected as uniform in nearly every cell.

Table~S8 decomposes the Brier score of the 95\% hit indicator. With a constant
forecast probability there is one bin, so reliability is the squared coverage gap
and resolution zero by construction, and the decomposition restates the coverage
table as a proper-score share with the same two-order-of-magnitude spread in both
regimes (under the break 0.1515 for Poisson Lee--Carter native against 0.0015 for
CNN-LC dropout). The PIT-scale reliability beside it, the distance from
uniformity a single level cannot see, orders the arms the same way (0.1904 down
to 0.0302 under the break; 0.1427 down to 0.0232 in the control) but never
approaches zero: the calibrated arms are calibrated at 95\%, not everywhere. The
isotonic (CORP) estimator is not emitted by the harness and no hypothesis rests
on it.

\subsection{Forecast comparison: Diebold--Mariano and the model confidence set}

Table~S9 carries the two registered inference procedures in all three regimes. A
contrast among distributional arms is decided on CRPS on log rates; any contrast
including a conformal arm is decided on the per-horizon 95\% interval score,
because a conformal cell's CRPS is a placeholder. The classical model confidence set is formed over the four full-age families
only: the five-family set including CBD is skipped in every regime, its reason
recorded, because a mean CRPS over 45 ages is not comparable with one over 100
and the registration forbids working around an incomparable loss. In the control,
on 297 common cells, the 90\% set retains the sparse VAR's native arm alone,
eliminating SVD-LC, Poisson Lee--Carter and APC/RH at $p=0.03$ each; under the
break, on 29 common cells, it retains \{SVD-LC native, sparse VAR native\}
(elimination $p$-values 0.13 and 1.00) and eliminates Poisson Lee--Carter and
APC/RH at $p<0.0005$---with the caveat that the sparse VAR is scored only on the
cells its own rejection rule admits. The set widens under the break because
thirteen origins of evidence are replaced by one, not because the eliminated
families improved.

The within-family conformal sets separate the wrappers far more sharply under the
break. In the control seven of the ten families retain all three wrappers, only
CBD and the sparse VAR reducing to EnbPI alone and the NB head to \{copula,
EnbPI\}; under the break EnbPI is the sole survivor in five families (SVD-LC,
LSTM-$\kappa_t$, NB head, neural-LC, sparse VAR), eliminating the split and
copula arms at $p$-values between 0.00 and 0.08, the copula arm survives alone in
Poisson Lee--Carter ($p=0.01$), and four families retain all three.

The Diebold--Mariano block prices the conformal repair and is the attribution
finding of the crossed design. It is defined for the seven families with a native
arm, and its sign is the same in the control as under the break, family by
family, without exception. Under the break split conformal is significantly
\emph{better} than the native law for exactly the four classical cores whose
native intervals broke---Poisson Lee--Carter ($\Delta=1.895$, $p=0.004$), APC/RH
($\Delta=1.039$, $p=0.013$), SVD-LC ($\Delta=0.700$, $p=0.018$), CBD
($\Delta=0.318$, $p=0.017$), $G=19$ clusters each---and significantly \emph{worse}
for exactly the three whose native law was already calibrated: NB head
($\Delta=-1.024$, $p<0.0002$, $G=19$), GP ($\Delta=-0.413$, $p=0.009$, $G=16$),
sparse VAR ($\Delta=-0.318$, $p<0.0002$, $G=14$). The control gives the same
seven signs: $+1.038$ for Poisson Lee--Carter ($p=0.032$), $+0.694$ for SVD-LC
($p=0.006$), $+0.451$ for APC/RH ($p=0.086$) and $+0.108$ for CBD ($p=0.198$),
against $-0.873$ for the NB head, $-0.772$ for the GP and $-0.236$ for the sparse
VAR, all three at $p\le0.0002$.

\subsection{Twin crises: 1914--1922 beside 2020--2024}

Table~S10 sets the placebo beside the shift regime. Read within regime (the joint
columns span $H=9$ against $H=5$), the placebo reproduces the shift regime's
family split a century earlier: the classical cores under-cover (Lee--Carter
0.787, Poisson Lee--Carter 0.780, APC/RH 0.761 marginal against a nominal 0.95),
the point-forecast neural mechanisms are worst (neural-LC ensemble 0.661, LSTM
0.636), the families that came through COVID calibrated sit near the top (NB head
0.849, GP 0.820), and joint path coverage collapses in the same order (0.146,
0.172, and 0.293--0.335 for the classical cores). \Hthree's gap is therefore not
a COVID artefact either, though over nine horizons its descriptive part is more
nearly arithmetic still ($0.95^9 = 0.630$): 45 of the 46 placebo arms beat
independence at their own marginal rate and 37 of the 46 nonetheless hold the
nine-year path less often than 0.630. The sparse VAR is the one family with no
placebo verdict under its native law and none under any conformal wrapper: the
pre-1914 native draws tripped the registered explosive-path rejection on all but
one cell per arm and that cell fails admissibility, leaving zero valid rows in
four of its five arms and emptying the common-cell intersection of the registered
classical confidence set here; only its Poisson-bootstrap arm survives, on nine
rows, at 0.969 marginal against 0.748 joint.

Against the control the placebo shows \Htwo's sorting from the other side, though
the two regimes share neither populations, era nor horizon count: every classical
core covers better through 1914--1922 than in the quiet 1990--2014 decades,
because a model estimated on the volatile pre-1914 panels produces much wider
bands---accidental calibration rather than a better law---while the arms
calibrated in the control lose the most (NB head 0.975 to 0.849, GP 0.945 to
0.820). Coverage again moves towards the middle from both directions. The
exception is CBD, near-nominal through the placebo break (0.986 marginal, 0.918
joint) and broken through the COVID one (0.799, 0.504): the 1918 influenza
concentrated its excess mortality at young-adult ages, below CBD's age-55
support, while COVID's excess fell at the old ages CBD models---\Hfour's
age-localisation operating \emph{between} regimes. The Addendum~1 strata sharpen
this: the neutral register-based populations hold the classical cores at
0.86--0.89, while the belligerent-total stratum, where war losses compound the
flu, drops the same families to 0.55--0.59, the civilian-only stratum in between.

The paired mechanism contrasts do not transfer. Native versus split is
insignificant on the interval score for every full-age classical core
(Lee--Carter $\Delta=-0.091$, $p=0.689$; Poisson Lee--Carter $p=0.127$; APC/RH
$p=0.563$), and the NB head's native law significantly beats its own wrapper
($\Delta=-0.461$, $p=0.008$): a calibration window drawn from the pre-1914 years
knows as little about the coming shock as the model it corrects. The
ensemble-versus-dropout verdicts flip family by family between the crises---CNN-LC,
indistinguishable under COVID ($p=0.739$), favours dropout here
($\Delta=+0.531$, $p=0.0002$); LSTM, which favours dropout under COVID
($p=0.0006$), is indistinguishable here ($p=0.478$). A family's fragility is
predictable from its past crisis; which uncertainty mechanism patches it best is
not.

\subsection{Architecture or mechanism? Contrasts within sub-grids}
\label{sec:results-contrasts}

Each contrast is pre-registered and computed as a paired difference within the
sub-grid named, in the control as well as under the break. Contrasts~2 and~3 have
no exhibit in either document---no ensemble-versus-dropout and no
bootstrap-versus-native row exists in Table~S9---so their statistics are reported
here in full.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Conformal versus native.} The Diebold--Mariano block above is this
  contrast, signed by the family and identical in sign in both regimes; the break
  changes the significance of two of the seven (CBD and APC/RH) and the magnitude
  of the repair for the Poisson families, but no family's side. The width side,
  from the interval-score column of Table~\ref{tab:h2-coverage-main}, is likewise
  regime-independent: for the broken families the wrapper buys coverage while
  \emph{shrinking} the score (Poisson Lee--Carter 3.056 native against 2.056
  split in the control, 4.456 against 2.674 under the break), for the calibrated
  ones it pays in width (NB head 1.455 against 2.335, and 1.684 against 2.717).
  Conformalisation is not a pandemic remedy.
\item \emph{Ensemble versus Monte Carlo dropout, neural families only.}
  Two mechanisms on one architecture do not give the same coverage---on CNN-LC
  dropout covers 0.962 against the ensemble's 0.882 under the break and 0.954
  against 0.863 in the control---and the registered paired test adds a sign the
  descriptive reading cannot supply: on the interval score the two differ significantly, \emph{in opposite
  directions}, on two of the four neural families under the break---dropout
  favoured on LSTM-on-$\kappa_t$ ($\Delta = +0.224$, $t = 3.78$, $p = 0.0006$),
  the ensemble on neural-LC ($\Delta = -1.238$, $t = -4.18$, $p < 0.0002$)---while
  CNN-LC ($p = 0.739$) and the NB head ($p = 0.139$) are indistinguishable. All
  four signs are the same in the control, on 434--442 common cells rather than
  38--40 (LSTM $+0.156$, $p=0.005$; neural-LC $-1.142$, $p=0.0002$; NB head
  $+0.049$, $p=0.019$; CNN-LC $+0.001$, $p=0.991$), so the opposing directions
  are a property of the family, not of the break. No single ordering of the two
  mechanisms survives a change of family, which is the crossed design's reason
  for existing.
\item \emph{Bootstrap versus native, classical families only.} Descriptively the
  movement toward nominal is positive and small in both regimes (SVD-LC 0.702 to
  0.726 in the control and 0.692 to 0.727 under the break; CBD 0.901 to 0.921 and
  0.799 to 0.830), nowhere near the conformal repair. The registered paired test
  makes the smallness precise: under the break the semiparametric bootstrap
  improves the interval score significantly on three of the five classical
  families---SVD-LC ($\Delta = -0.112$, $p < 0.0002$), Poisson Lee--Carter
  ($\Delta = -0.115$, $p < 0.0002$), Renshaw--Haberman ($\Delta = -0.101$,
  $p = 0.0002$)---leaves CBD unchanged ($p = 0.681$) and significantly
  \emph{worsens} the sparse VAR ($\Delta = +0.168$, $p = 0.0008$, on the 22 cells
  where its native arm survives the explosive-draw rejection of Addendum~3~\S7).
  The control gives the same three improvements at the same order of magnitude
  ($-0.100$, $-0.073$, $-0.075$, each at $p \le 0.0002$, on 442 cells) and the
  same sparse VAR penalty ($+0.237$, $p < 0.0002$, on 223 cells), with CBD
  favouring its native arm there ($+0.096$, $p = 0.016$). These improvements are
  an order of magnitude smaller than the conformal repair of the same families
  (Poisson Lee--Carter, $-0.115$ against $+1.895$ for the wrapper): resampling
  the estimator moves a broken interval but does not fix it.
\item \emph{Joint-path conformal versus split conformal, all families.} The
  \Hthree\ mechanism contrast: the conformal confidence sets above read with the
  joint column of Table~\ref{tab:h3-joint-main}. The copula arm holds joint path
  coverage of 0.894--0.942 in the control and 0.805--0.927 under the break where
  the split arm holds 0.760--0.842 and 0.692--0.862, yet on the single-level
  interval score it survives as sole set member only for Poisson Lee--Carter
  under the break and is eliminated in five families where EnbPI's narrower band
  wins. A practitioner who needs the path pays for it at the level; the study
  reports the price rather than adjudicating it.
\end{enumerate}
