\section{Study design}
\label{sec:design}

\subsection{Pre-registration and its addenda}
\label{sec:design-prereg}

The design was committed and hash-stamped on 2026-08-25, before any model was
fit to real HMD data; its SHA-256 digest is
\begin{center}
\small\texttt{bbbe3860a5446d063e186e56555afa893b07c36cf15a990d07567471affe50be}
\end{center}
It fixes regimes, populations, families, mechanisms, grid, metrics and their
conventions, inference, gates and hypotheses. Pre-registration protects against
analytic flexibility, not against hypothesis surprise: it could not hide
2020--2024 from us. Three addenda followed on 2026-08-26, hash-stamped in their
own commits and still before any real-data fit; none changes a population, split
year, horizon, observation or metric. They add the placebo strata and
sensitivities of Section~\ref{sec:data}, fix the scoring target, pin computation
left implicit, and record the hypothesis statuses below before any result
existed. All three, with the hash ledger, are Supplementary Section~S1; a fourth
is in Section~\ref{sec:design-deviations}.

The \emph{stable} regime measures calibration where nothing breaks: the
reference level for \Htwo\ and \Hfive, and a replication arm for the settled
half of \Htwo\ (Section~\ref{sec:related}). The \emph{shift} regime is the
audit; the \emph{placebo} regime reads a second event---the First World War and
the 1918 influenza---beside 2020--2024, answering ``is this just COVID?''
without a transfer regression. Windows are expanding-origin, never random:
training takes the maximal contiguous block of complete years ending at the
origin, and a triple needs fifteen such years to be admissible
(Addendum~3~\S2,~\S4; Supplementary Section~S2).

All twenty populations clear that floor under the shift; in the stable regime it
is no marginal trim. HRV and KOR clear it at no origin, so the control covers
eighteen; CHL is admissible at five of thirteen origins, HKG at eight; 221 of
260 (origin, population) pairs---442 of 520 triples---clear it, sixteen
populations at origins 1990--1998 and eighteen from 2006. Stable summaries are
read beside their per-origin count, contrasts with the shift on the
eighteen-population intersection (Addendum~3~\S4,~\S11).

\subsection{Hypothesis status after the prior-art map}
\label{sec:design-status}

The five hypotheses of Section~\ref{sec:intro} are tested as registered;
Addendum~3~\S8 records, before any result existed, how the prior-art map changes
their reading. \Hone\ becomes a harness-validity check---the
point-versus-proper-score inversion is published three times over
(Section~\ref{sec:related})---its magnitude, per population and unweighted by
exposure, being what is new. \Htwo\ is restated \emph{two-sided}, the tested
quantity being the absolute departure of coverage from nominal with its
direction free by cell, because the registered one-sided form, under-coverage
``for every model family'', is already contradicted in the stable regime by
bootstrap-ensemble neural intervals. \Hthree's comparator is re-specified: the
registered one, the marginal rate at the same nominal level, equals
$0.95^5=0.774$ under independence and made the comparison close to
arithmetically guaranteed; it is replaced by each cell's own
simulated-path-implied joint coverage, which is recomputable from the predictive
paths but is not among the columns the runner emitted, so \Hthree\ carries no
registered verdict (Section~\ref{sec:metrics}). \Hfour's registered old-age
direction is contradicted by \citet{dowd2010backtesting}, the reversal
pre-committed as informative rather than re-tested until confirmation. \Hfive\
is restricted to the ex-post coverage of the derived intervals $e_0$, $e_{65}$
and $\annuity$, whose construction is prior art, against
\citet{cairns2011mortality}'s attenuation finding: the test is of calibration
error propagating, not of point applications dispersing.

\subsection{Model families}
\label{sec:design-families}

Ten families span the classical, statistical-learning and neural lineages
(Section~\ref{sec:related}), each fit per population and sex over ages 0--99
(CBD: 55--99) to the origin under the weights $W=\ind\{E>0\}$ of
Section~\ref{sec:data}: \textbf{Lee--Carter} \citep{lee1992modeling},
\textbf{Poisson Lee--Carter} \citep{brouhns2002poisson}, \textbf{CBD (M5)}
\citep{cairns2006two}, \textbf{APC / Renshaw--Haberman (M2-A)}
\citep{renshaw2006cohort}, an age-banded \textbf{sparse VAR}(1) on improvements
after \citet{li2017coherent}, the \textbf{multi-output GP} of
\citet{huynh2021multi}, \textbf{neural-LC} \citep{richman2021neural},
\textbf{shallow CNN-LC} \citep{perla2021time}, \textbf{LSTM-on-$\kappa_t$}
\citep{nigri2019deep}, and a \textbf{distributional NB head} on the neural-LC
features. Specifications, losses, constraints and hyperparameter grids are
Supplementary Section~S2; neural and GP hyperparameters tune on an inner
\emph{time} split, never on a test year or on coverage.

CBD is a registered exception carried into every table below: fitted only on
ages 55--99, where the logit is near-linear, and undefined beneath, it is scored
on 45 ages, kept in its own row block with that support stated, never averaged
into a ranking with families scored on 100 ages, and its ``25--64'' column is a
55--64 stub.

\subsection{Uncertainty mechanisms}
\label{sec:design-mechanisms}

Seven mechanisms turn a fitted family into predictive sample paths on the rate
scale (Supplementary Section~S2): \textbf{model-native}; the
\textbf{semiparametric Poisson bootstrap}, 200 refits to Poisson pseudo-deaths
\citep{brouhns2005bootstrapping}; a \textbf{deep ensemble} of ten seeded
trainings \citep{lakshminarayanan2017simple}; \textbf{Monte Carlo dropout}
\citep{gal2016dropout}; \textbf{split conformal} on the log-rate scale,
Mondrian-stratified by age band and horizon
\citep{lei2018distribution,vovk2005algorithmic}; \textbf{EnbPI}
\citep{xu2021conformal}, with four documented deviations for annual multi-step
forecasting; and \textbf{copula / joint-path conformal}, a simultaneous band
from a supremum-norm score on the same ten member residuals
\citep{diquigiovanni2022conformal,sun2024copula,messoudi2021copula}, the only
mechanism calibrated to be joint over the whole path. Registered in advance:
ensemble mixtures are narrow, and under-coverage there is a finding about the
mechanism, not a defect to widen away.

Four registered disciplines govern the conformal arms (Addendum~2~\S3;
Addendum~3~\S6,~\S9). No calibration residual comes from a test year, and the
calibration horizon is nine in every regime. A wrapper yields an interval, not a
distribution, so its coverage, width, interval score and joint path coverage are
read from its own 95\% bounds, its 50\% and 80\% columns are reported \emph{not
applicable}, and its proper scores---from draws uniform inside the
interval---are placeholders flagged \texttt{secondary}, never ranked against
distributional mechanisms (Supplementary Section~S5). Contrasts including a
conformal arm are decided on the 95\% interval score
(Section~\ref{sec:metrics}).

\subsection{The crossed grid}
\label{sec:design-grid}

Family and mechanism are crossed as separate factors, which is what makes this a
study rather than a bake-off: crossing separates bad coverage as a property of
the architecture from bad coverage as a property of the mechanism bolted on. Not
every pair is admissible---deterministic fits have no seed variance, and no
network but the NB head has a closed-form native predictive---so
Table~\ref{tab:grid} carries 50 primary cells and four secondary, the latter in
the supplement only. Conformal columns do not reuse the native fit: every
wrapper refits the base family (Supplementary Section~S2).

Design floors belong to the family--mechanism pair, a wrapper's inner fits
inheriting the base family's minimum (Supplementary Sections~S2 and~S6). The
GP's forty years is much the highest---a shorter panel leaves its time-kernel
length-scale unidentified against the nugget---and that floor, not the
fifteen-year admissibility rule, removes CHL, HKG, HRV and KOR from every GP
cell, so the GP is read on sixteen populations where the other nine are read on
twenty (Table~S1).

The grid being ragged, no claim here is a full-factorial main effect: every
comparison is a contrast within an admissible sub-grid, computed on the cells
common to the arms compared, with that count printed beside it so that no
denominator silently omits an error row (Section~\ref{sec:metrics};
Addendum~3~\S11).

\subsection{One interface, one scoring path}
\label{sec:design-interface}

Every cell implements the same two calls---a fit on deaths and exposures, and a
sampler returning pathwise trajectories of future rates, paths rather than
marginals, which is why joint path coverage is computable---and one evaluation
module scores them all, the pointwise median serving every point metric. Scoring
is \emph{Poisson-inclusive}: each path draws a Poisson count at its own rate and
the test-year exposure, so the law scored is that of the rate actually observed
rather than of the latent rate (Addendum~2~\S1; Supplementary Section~S2).

\subsection{Seeds, validation gates and inference}
\label{sec:design-gates}

The global seed is 20260825; every cell's generator derives from it, and seeds,
device, training window, cell counts and any exception string are recorded per
row. Three gates passed before any real-data result was read (Supplementary
Section~S1): on synthetic truth from a known Poisson Lee--Carter process, run
through the real-data code path, the correctly specified model attains nominal
95\% coverage with a near-uniform PIT and the harness flags an injected break as
under-coverage; on Swedish males, 1950--2000, ages 0--99, our Poisson
Lee--Carter and R \pkg{StMoMo} 0.4.1 have identical Poisson log-likelihood and
maximum relative differences of $3.1\times10^{-15}$, $2.4\times10^{-14}$ and
$1.5\times10^{-13}$ in $\alpha_x$, $\beta_x$ and $\kappa_t$; and our period life
table reproduces HMD's published $e_0$ and $e_{65}$ within 0.15~years. Inference
treats the shift regime as one event---twenty populations, one common shock---so
the population is the cluster and the placebo a second event, not more draws
from the same one (Section~\ref{sec:metrics}).

\subsection{Deviations from the pre-registration}
\label{sec:design-deviations}

\begin{itemize}
\item Ordering: \citet{dowd2010backtesting} and \citet{schnurch2022point} were
  read on 2026-08-26, after the registration was frozen and before any real-data
  fit; nothing in the frozen design depends on either, and the consequences are
  registered in Addendum~3~\S8 and stated above. Addenda~1--3 are not
  deviations.
\item CNN learning-rate grid lowered before the first real-data run, the
  registered grid having diverged at every point on synthetic data; neural
  gate~G-N1 recalibrated to a finite bound on point error from a
  within-twice-the-Poisson-Lee--Carter-error form, the networks' extrapolation
  plateau being the audited fragility rather than an implementation defect. Both
  dated in \texttt{docs/NEURAL-SPEC.md}.
\item Seed derivation. Registered: member seed equals the global seed plus the
  member index. Implemented: each member is seeded from a child of the cell's
  seed sequence---distinct, deterministic and recorded per row, but without the
  collisions an additive offset produces between neighbouring cells. No result
  depends on the integers; the code does not do literally what the registered
  sentence says.
\item Training window of the multi-output GP (Addendum~4, 2026-08-29, registered
  during the sweeps, before any GP cell on a panel longer than sixty years had
  produced a result). Registered: the whole trailing block of complete years;
  implemented: the trailing sixty, under every mechanism. Kernel memory is
  quadratic in years times ages---a 269-year panel is 26{,}900 points and a
  5.8~GB kernel per fit---and with ten member refits per cell the second-pass
  sweep stalled after nineteen hours at 10.7~GB in one worker, fourteen of
  twenty populations untouched. Sixty is the longest panel the arm had completed
  uncapped, so no computed cell changes, and it exceeds twice
  \citet{huynh2021multi}'s 27-year window. Disclosure: before any cap was
  contemplated, one diagnostic on one population had shown a trailing
  forty-year window scoring better than the full panel; the cap is sixty, not
  forty, and was not selected by score.
\item The ledger is open: any further deviation is appended with its date and
  reason, so that at submission the absence of further entries is a positive
  claim, not a default.
\end{itemize}
