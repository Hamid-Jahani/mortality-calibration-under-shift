\section{Study design}
\label{sec:design}

\subsection{Pre-registration and its addenda}
\label{sec:design-prereg}

The design was committed and hash-stamped on 2026-08-25, before any model was
fit to real HMD data; its SHA-256 digest is
\begin{center}
\small\texttt{bbbe3860a5446d063e186e56555afa893b07c36cf15a990d07567471affe50be}
\end{center}
It fixes the regimes, populations, families, mechanisms and admissible grid,
every metric and its convention, the inference procedure, the gates and the five
hypotheses. It could not hide the outcome of 2020--2024 from us:
pre-registration here protects against analytic flexibility, not against
hypothesis surprise. Three addenda followed on 2026-08-26, each hash-stamped in
its own commit and each still before any real-data fit, and none changes a
population list, a split year, a horizon, a test-window observation or the
metric list: they add the placebo strata and denominator sensitivities of
Section~\ref{sec:data} (\texttt{5f2e3cb}), fix the scoring target
(\texttt{577ce52}), and specify computation the registration covers but does not
spell out---the admissibility floor, rejection of explosive VAR draws, the
common-cell rule---while recording, before any result existed, the hypothesis
statuses below (\texttt{01f154a}). Each clause is cited where it applies, and
the addenda in full are Supplementary Section~S1; a fourth, registered during
the production sweeps, is in Section~\ref{sec:design-deviations}.

The \emph{stable} regime measures calibration where nothing breaks: the
reference level for \Htwo\ and \Hfive\ and, its half of \Htwo\ being settled
already (Section~\ref{sec:related}), a replication arm. The \emph{shift} regime
is the audit. The \emph{placebo} regime is a second event---the First World War
and the 1918 influenza pandemic---read descriptively, a side-by-side of
1914--1922 and 2020--2024 coverage answering ``is this just COVID?'' without a
transfer regression. Windows are expanding-origin, never random, since mortality
panels leak trivially under random splitting; training takes the maximal
contiguous block of complete years ending at the origin, and a triple is
admissible only with fifteen such years (Addendum~3~\S2, \S4), inadmissible
triples being recorded and not scored.

All twenty registered populations clear that floor in the shift regime. In the
stable regime it is no marginal trim: HRV's series begins in 2001 and KOR's in
2003, so even their best origin ($T=2014$) yields fourteen and twelve training
years, and the stable control covers eighteen of the twenty. CHL (from 1992) is
admissible at five of the thirteen origins and HKG (1986) at eight; 221 of the
260 (origin, population) pairs---442 of the 520 triples---clear the floor, and
the panel is unbalanced across origins, sixteen populations at origins
1990--1998 and eighteen from 2006. Every stable-regime summary is therefore read
beside its per-origin population count (Addendum~3~\S4), and every
stable-versus-shift contrast is computed on the eighteen-population intersection
(Addendum~3~\S11), never a twenty-population mean against an eighteen-population
one.

\subsection{Hypothesis status after the prior-art map}
\label{sec:design-status}

The five hypotheses of Section~\ref{sec:intro} are reproduced verbatim from the
registration and tested as registered; Addendum~3~\S8 records, before any result
existed, how the prior-art map changes their reading. \Hone\ is demoted from
headline finding to \emph{harness-validity check}, the point-versus-proper-score
inversion being published three times over (Section~\ref{sec:related}): ours is
its magnitude, per population and unweighted by exposure, so that it cannot be
the population-size artefact \citet{schnurch2022point} describe.
\Htwo\ is restated \emph{two-sided}---the tested quantity is
$|\text{coverage}-\text{nominal}|$, hypothesised to grow from the stable to the
shift regime, its direction free to differ by cell---because the registered
one-sided form, under-coverage ``for every model family'', is already
contradicted in the stable regime by bootstrap-ensemble neural intervals.
\Hthree's comparator is re-specified: realised joint path coverage is compared
with each cell's \emph{own simulated-path-implied} joint coverage, computable
from the same predictive paths, and not with the marginal rate at the same
nominal level, because joint coverage is bounded above by the minimum marginal
coverage and equals $0.95^5=0.774$ under independence, so the registered
comparison was close to arithmetically guaranteed; the realised-minus-implied
gap can be of either sign. \Hfour's registered direction (worse at 65--99 than
at 25--64) is contradicted by \citet{dowd2010backtesting}; we pre-commit to
reporting a reversal as an informative result, not to re-testing until
confirmation, and note that the 0--24 band, infant mortality and the 90+ tail
are unmeasured in prior work. \Hfive\ is restricted to the \emph{ex-post
coverage} of derived-quantity intervals ($e_0$, $e_{65}$, $\annuity$), whose
construction is prior art; the named alternative is
\citet{cairns2011mortality}'s attenuation finding, so the test is of calibration
error propagating, not of point applications dispersing.

\subsection{Model families}
\label{sec:design-families}

Ten families span the classical, statistical-learning and neural lineages of
Section~\ref{sec:related}, each fit per population and sex on deaths and
exposures over ages $0$--$99$ (CBD: $55$--$99$) and years to the origin, under
the cell weights $W=\ind\{E>0\}$ of Section~\ref{sec:data}:
\textbf{Lee--Carter} by weighted SVD with a drift-uncertain random walk on
$\kappa_t$ \citep{lee1992modeling}; \textbf{Poisson Lee--Carter}, the same
structure under a Poisson likelihood with log-exposure offset
\citep{brouhns2002poisson}; \textbf{CBD (M5)} \citep{cairns2006two}; \textbf{APC
/ Renshaw--Haberman (M2-A)}, a cohort term under the constraints of
\citet{cairns2009quantitative} \citep{renshaw2006cohort}; an age-banded
\textbf{sparse VAR}(1) on improvements, simplifying \citet{li2017coherent}; the
\textbf{multi-output GP} of \citet{huynh2021multi}, exact multitask with an
$\mathrm{RBF}(\text{year})\otimes\mathrm{ICM}$ kernel, whose predictive is the
joint posterior over the $h$ future years and whose Kronecker structure is the
source of the forty-year floor below; \textbf{neural-LC}, the age-embedding
network of \citet{richman2021neural}, whose extrapolation of the year feature is
the audited fragility rather than a defect to engineer away; \textbf{shallow
CNN-LC} \citep{perla2021time}; \textbf{LSTM-on-$\kappa_t$} \citep{nigri2019deep}
in place of the random walk; and a \textbf{distributional NB head} on the
neural-LC features, the only neural family with a model-native predictive
distribution. Losses are the Poisson deviance for the three point networks, NB2
for the head, Gaussian marginal likelihood for the GP, and each classical
family's own criterion; architectures and hyperparameter grids in full are
Supplementary Section~S2 and \texttt{docs/NEURAL-SPEC.md}, committed before the
first real-data fit.

Three conventions are registered, not descriptive. CBD is fitted only on ages
55--99, where the logit is near-linear, and is undefined below: it is scored on
45 ages, kept in its own row block with that support stated, never averaged into
a ranking with families scored on 100 ages, and its ``25--64'' column is a
55--64 stub not comparable with the full-age families. A VAR draw whose
companion matrix has spectral radius $\ge1$ is rejected and redrawn, up to a
hundred times per path, and rates are never clipped to rescue a divergent draw,
since clipping would turn divergence into a bounded interval and flatter the
family's coverage (Addendum~3~\S7). The NB head's rate samples are gamma draws,
so the Poisson composition below reproduces the NB count law exactly, without
double counting. Neural and GP hyperparameters are tuned on an inner \emph{time}
split, the final five pre-cutoff years, by the family's own loss---never on a
test year, a random subset or coverage.

\subsection{Uncertainty mechanisms}
\label{sec:design-mechanisms}

Seven mechanisms turn a fitted family into predictive sample paths on the $m_x$
scale: \textbf{model-native}, the predictive the reference paper supplies; the
\textbf{semiparametric Poisson bootstrap}, $B=200$ refits to pseudo-deaths
$D^{(b)}\sim\mathrm{Poisson}(E\hat m)$ pooled over their native samples
\citep{brouhns2005bootstrapping}; a \textbf{deep ensemble} of $M=10$
independently seeded trainings, paths drawn with equal weight from each member's
predictive \citep{lakshminarayanan2017simple}; \textbf{Monte Carlo dropout},
dropout kept active at prediction time \citep{gal2016dropout}; \textbf{split
conformal}, absolute-residual intervals on the log-rate scale
\citep{lei2018distribution} Mondrian-stratified by age band ($0$--$24$,
$25$--$64$, $65$--$99$) and horizon \citep{vovk2005algorithmic}, with the last
nine training years held out for calibration; \textbf{EnbPI}
\citep{xu2021conformal}, adapted to annual multi-step forecasting with four
documented deviations (Supplementary Section~S2), among them $K=10$ members at
origins one year apart and horizon-matched residuals; and \textbf{copula /
joint-path conformal}, a simultaneous band from a supremum-norm score on those
same member residuals \citep{diquigiovanni2022conformal}, after
\citet{sun2024copula} and \citet{messoudi2021copula}, the only mechanism
calibrated to be joint over $h=1,\dots,H$. Stated in advance about the ensemble:
such mixtures are narrow, and if they under-cover that is a finding about the
mechanism, not a defect to widen away.

Conformal arms carry four registered disciplines. Calibration residuals come
only from years at or before the training cutoff and later than the base fit's
own training years, so no mechanism sees a test year, and the calibration
horizon is nine in every regime, at least the longest scored horizon
(Addendum~3~\S9). A wrapper yields an interval, not a distribution: coverage,
width, interval score and joint path coverage are read from its own bounds at
its own construction level, 95\%, with no Poisson composition---the radius
already contains observation noise---and no quantile-of-samples step
(Addendum~2~\S3, Addendum~3~\S6). Coverage and interval scores at 50\% and 80\%
are \emph{not applicable} to conformal cells and are reported as such. And
because the harness preserves one sample interface by drawing uniformly inside
each interval, CRPS, log score and PIT for those cells describe a placeholder
density: they are flagged \texttt{secondary}, released per cell with the results
files and described in Supplementary Section~S5, and never ranked against
distributional mechanisms. Any contrast including a conformal arm is decided on
the 95\% interval score (Section~\ref{sec:metrics}).

\subsection{The crossed grid}
\label{sec:design-grid}

Family and mechanism are crossed as separate factors, which is what makes this a
study rather than a bake-off: crossing separates bad coverage as a property of
the architecture from bad coverage as a property of the mechanism bolted on. Not
every pair is admissible---deterministic classical fits have no seed variance,
the GP posterior already integrates parameter uncertainty, and networks have no
closed-form native predictive but the NB head. Table~\ref{tab:grid} enumerates
50 primary cells and four secondary (the GP under a deep ensemble, the three
point-forecast networks under the Poisson bootstrap), the secondary reported in
the supplement only. The conformal columns do \emph{not} reuse the native fit:
each wrapper refits the base family, twice for split conformal and eleven times
for EnbPI and the copula band.

\paragraph{Design-floor cells.} A floor belongs to the family--mechanism pair, a
wrapper's inner fits inheriting the base family's minimum: ten training years
for every family, more than fifteen for the CNN and more than seventeen for the
LSTM, with split conformal fitting its base on $T-9$ after nine held-out
calibration years and EnbPI and the copula band fitting ten members one year
apart, their earliest seeing $T-18$. The GP's floor is much the highest, forty
years, a shorter panel leaving its time-kernel length-scale unidentified against
the nugget; that floor---not the fifteen-year admissibility rule---removes CHL,
HKG, HRV and KOR from every GP cell in shift and stable regimes alike, so the GP
family is read on sixteen populations where the other nine are read on twenty.
Among the shift panels only the four shortest lose cells---KOR (seventeen
years), HRV (nineteen), CHL (twenty-eight), HKG (thirty-four). These are
properties of the design, not failures of a fit, and are tabulated cell by cell,
with the longer stable-regime list, in Supplementary Section~S6 (Tables~S11
and~S12).

\paragraph{Contrasts on common cells.} Because the grid is ragged, no claim here
is a full-factorial main effect. Every comparison is a \emph{contrast within an
admissible sub-grid}---conformal versus native and bootstrap versus native
within the classical families, ensemble versus dropout within the neural
families, joint-path versus split conformal across all---computed on the
\emph{intersection} of cells in which each compared arm produced a valid row,
with the intersection size printed beside it; uncensored per-arm tables are in
the supplement, and no denominator silently omits an error row
(Addendum~3~\S11). A quality gate classes every missing row before any number is
read, as a \emph{machine} failure (blocking, re-run), a \emph{design-floor} cell
as above, or a \emph{method} failure where the registered procedure refuses to
forecast---the last a property of the family, shrinking its arms. Error rows
enter no mean.

\subsection{One interface, one scoring path}
\label{sec:design-interface}

Every cell implements the same two calls, $\texttt{fit}(D,E)$ and
$\texttt{sample\_mx}(h,n)$, returning $n$ pathwise trajectories of
$m_{x,T+1},\dots,m_{x,T+h}$. The scoring module makes
every distributional cell \emph{Poisson-inclusive}: on each path it draws
$D^{\ast(j)}_{x,h}\sim\mathrm{Poisson}(E_{x,T+h}\,m^{(j)}_{x,T+h})$ and forms
the half-count log crude rate, so the object scored is the predictive law of the
rate actually observed (Addendum~2~\S1). Scoring a latent-rate sample against an
observed crude rate instead would count Poisson observation noise as
miscalibration, worst at young ages and in small populations: before this was
fixed, a correctly specified Poisson Lee--Carter covered a fraction of nominal
at every level on synthetic truth. Every point forecast, interval, proper score,
PIT value, joint path coverage and life-table functional comes from these
samples through one evaluation module, so no model can be flattered by a bespoke
metric implementation. One point functional serves every point metric: the
pointwise median, of the log rate for RMSE and MAE and of the per-sample
life-table functionals for $e_0$, $e_{65}$ and $\annuity$. No column uses the
mean.

\subsection{Seeds, validation gates and inference}
\label{sec:design-gates}

The global seed is 20260825; each cell derives its generator from a seed
sequence over its key, and seeds, device, training window, cell counts and any
exception string are recorded per row. Three gates had to pass before any
real-data result could be read, and all three did (detail in Supplementary
Section~S2; gates~1 and~2 were re-run after the weighted-fit rewrite of
Addendum~3~\S1). On synthetic truth simulated from a known Poisson Lee--Carter
process and pushed through the real-data code path, the correctly specified
model attains nominal 95\% coverage within Monte Carlo tolerance with a
near-uniform PIT and the harness flags an injected break as under-coverage; each
neural and GP family passed its own gate on the same worlds. On Swedish males,
1950--2000, ages $0$--$99$, our Poisson Lee--Carter and R \pkg{StMoMo} 0.4.1
have identical Poisson log-likelihood and maximum relative differences of
$3.1\times10^{-15}$ in $\alpha_x$, $2.4\times10^{-14}$ in $\beta_x$ and
$1.5\times10^{-13}$ in $\kappa_t$; and our period life table reproduces HMD's
published $e_0$ and $e_{65}$ within 0.15~years (Section~\ref{sec:data}). The synthetic
gate also documents a limitation: two-stage SVD Lee--Carter is mildly
\emph{over}-dispersed on Poisson data, its SVD $\kappa_t$ carrying observation
noise into the random-walk innovation variance, so its intervals are slightly
wide and its PIT centre-heavy---a property of the method, not the harness,
carried into the reading of every SVD-LC row. Inference treats the shift regime
as one event: twenty populations experienced one common shock, so the effective
number of independent observations of ``what a pandemic does to an interval'' is
nearer one than twenty. Population is the cluster, every test resamples clusters
rather than cells and imposes the null before resampling, and the placebo is a
second event rather than more draws from the same one
(Section~\ref{sec:metrics}).

\subsection{Deviations from the pre-registration}
\label{sec:design-deviations}

\begin{itemize}
\item Ordering: \citet{dowd2010backtesting} and \citet{schnurch2022point} were
  read on 2026-08-26, after the registration was frozen and before any real-data
  fit; the consequences are registered in Addendum~3~\S8 and stated in
  Section~\ref{sec:design-status}. Nothing in the frozen design depends on
  either paper.
\item Addenda~1--3 (2026-08-26) are listed for completeness, not as deviations:
  no population, split, horizon, observation or metric changed.
\item CNN learning-rate grid lowered before the first real-data run, the
  registered grid having diverged at every point on synthetic data; neural
  gate~G-N1 recalibrated to finite $\mathrm{RMSE}(\log m)<1$ over $h=1\ldots5$
  from a within-twice-the-Poisson-Lee--Carter-error form, the cell-feature
  networks' extrapolation plateau being the audited fragility rather than an
  implementation defect. Both dated in \texttt{docs/NEURAL-SPEC.md}.
\item Seed derivation. Registered: member seed $=$ global seed $+$ member index.
  Implemented: member $j$'s seed is the $j$-th child of the cell's
  \texttt{SeedSequence}, whose entropy is the global seed plus stable hashes of
  the cell key, so any cell re-runs in isolation and reproduces its row. Seeds
  stay distinct, deterministic and recorded per row, as the registration wanted,
  without the collisions an additive offset produces between neighbouring cells;
  no result depends on the integers, but the code does not do literally what the
  registered sentence says.
\item Training window of the multi-output GP (Addendum~4, 2026-08-29, registered
  during the production sweeps, before any GP cell on a panel longer than sixty
  years had produced a result). Registered: the whole trailing block of complete
  years. Implemented: the trailing sixty, under every mechanism. Cause: kernel
  memory scales with $(\text{years}\times\text{ages})^2$---a 269-year panel is
  26{,}900 points and a 5.8~GB kernel per fit, and EnbPI and copula conformal
  refit the family ten times per cell---so the second-pass sweep stalled after
  nineteen hours with one worker at 10.7~GB, the machine swapping and fourteen
  of twenty populations untouched. Sixty is the longest panel the arm had
  completed uncapped, so no computed cell changes, and it exceeds twice the
  27-year window of the source paper \citep{huynh2021multi}. Disclosure: before
  any cap was contemplated, a single diagnostic on one population had shown a
  trailing forty-year window scoring better than the full panel; the cap chosen
  is sixty, not forty, and was not selected by score.
\item The ledger is open while the stable-control and placebo analyses run: any
  further deviation is appended with its date and reason, so that at submission
  the absence of further entries is a positive claim that none arose, not a
  default.
\end{itemize}
