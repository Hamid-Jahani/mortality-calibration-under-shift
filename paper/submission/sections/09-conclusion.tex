\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Ten forecasting families crossed with seven uncertainty mechanisms gave fifty
admissible cells over three registered regimes: a stable control of thirteen
expanding origins, 1990--2014; the shift regime, built from pre-2020 data and
scored on realised 2020--2024 mortality in twenty populations (sixteen for the
multi-output GP, whose forty-year training floor removes four short panels and
whose window Addendum~4 caps at sixty years); and the 1914--1922 placebo,
eleven populations over nine horizons. No excluded row is a machine failure;
all fail a registered admissibility rule (Tables~S11 and~S12).

The control changes the question the title asks: most miscalibration predates
the pandemic. With no break in its test windows, native 95\% coverage runs
0.682 (Poisson Lee--Carter) to 0.731 (APC/RH), the LSTM-$\kappa_t$ deep
ensemble 0.638 (Table~S3).

\Hone, a registered harness-validity check, is supported: RMSE and CRPS
disagree at the registered top-three inversion, and against the Poisson log
score point accuracy dissolves ($\rho=0.12$ break, $-0.18$ control; Table~S2,
note).

\Htwo\ fails as registered: the one-sided clause is unsupported, since a fall
is degradation only for an arm that began below nominal. Marginal coverage
falls from control to break in 34 of 50 cells, but the two-sided quantity
actually tested, $|\text{coverage}-\text{nominal}|$ (Addendum~3), grows in only
27 of 50 (CNN-LC under split conformal rounds to 0.932 in both regimes; counted
unrounded). The break sorts the grid by which side of nominal each arm began on
(sparse VAR 0.988 to 0.937, Poisson Lee--Carter 0.682 to 0.624); without the
control all 34 read as degradation (Table~S3).

\Hthree\ needs care. Joint path coverage lies below marginal in all 100
(regime, cell) pairs of the two five-horizon regimes---mean gap 0.127 control,
0.168 break, 0.362 over nine placebo horizons---but independence alone opens
$0.95-0.95^5=0.176$. Against each arm's own implied independence,
$\text{marginal}^H$, joint coverage \emph{exceeds} the benchmark in 50 of 50
control, 50 of 50 shift and 45 of 46 placebo cells (exception: the sparse VAR
under the Poisson bootstrap) by up to 0.344: horizons are strongly positively
dependent, which \citet{dowd2010backtesting} name but do not measure. The
registered Addendum~3 comparator, each cell's model-implied joint coverage, is
not computable from the emitted columns; no verdict is reported against it.
Only the copula band, built over the path, holds (0.894--0.942 control,
0.805--0.927 break); over nine placebo horizons nothing does, it included
(0.544--0.939). Table~S4.

\Hfour\ is refuted as a universal: the old-age concentration belongs to the
index extrapolators and the LSTM (Section~\ref{sec:discussion-split}). SVD
Lee--Carter natively covers 0.888, 0.760 and 0.501 across ages 0--24, 25--64
and 65--99 in the control---a gradient of four tenths with no break
present---while calibrated arms are near-flat and three reverse at the break
(65--99 above 25--64, margins under 0.05 discarded): CBD natively and under the
bootstrap, neural-LC under dropout. CBD is fitted on ages 55--99, so its 25--64
column is a 55--64 stub, not comparable with the full-age families (Table~S5).

\Hfive\ is the severest result: calibration does not survive integration across
ages. In the control SVD Lee--Carter natively covers 0.253 of its nominal 95\%
$\annuity$ intervals and 0.256 for $e_{65}$, and the LSTM deep ensemble 0.157
for $\annuity$---an arm with rate-level marginal coverage 0.702 missing three
annuity intervals in four in a quiet period (Table~S6).

Split conformal significantly improves the 95\% interval score of families
whose native law was broken and worsens those whose was sound: mean
$\IS_{0.05}$ differences of $+0.694$ control and $+0.700$ break for SVD
Lee--Carter against $-0.873$ and $-1.024$ for the NB head, in five of seven
families in the control (CBD $p=0.198$, APC/RH $p=0.086$: null) and all seven
at the break. Over nine placebo horizons all six estimates favour the native
law instead, SVD Lee--Carter at $-0.091$ (Table~S9). Wrap what is broken; do
not wrap what is calibrated.

The remaining contribution is the protocol: a pre-registration hash-stamped
before the first fit; two validation gates, nominal coverage on synthetic truth
and reference-implementation parity to machine precision; cluster bootstrap
inference treating a pandemic as one event rather than thousands of cells; and
the matched control this study could not do without. Most of these intervals were
never calibrated.
