\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Mortality prediction intervals are priced and reserved against, yet none had
been scored against a realised structural break beside a matched
control. This audit crossed ten forecasting families with seven uncertainty
mechanisms over fifty admissible cells and three registered regimes: a stable
control of thirteen expanding origins, 1990--2014; the shift regime, built strictly
from data through 2019 and scored against realised 2020--2024 mortality
in twenty populations (sixteen for the multi-output GP, whose Addendum-4
forty-year training-window floor excludes four short panels); and the
1914--1922 placebo, eleven populations over nine horizons. No machine-failure
row appears among the 6{,}480 / 210 / 136 excluded rows of the three sweeps
(Tables~S11 and~S12; those exclusions are registered-rule admissibility
failures, not broken runs).

The control changes the question the title asks: most of the miscalibration
predates the pandemic. With no break in its test windows, the classical
cores under their own predictive law cover 0.682 (Poisson
Lee--Carter), 0.702 (SVD Lee--Carter) and 0.731 (APC/RH) against a nominal
95\%, and the LSTM-$\kappa_t$ deep ensemble 0.638 (Table~S3).

\Hone, a registered harness-validity check, is supported: RMSE and CRPS
disagree at the registered top-three inversion, and against the Poisson
log score the point-accuracy ordering dissolves, $\rho=0.12$ at the break and
$-0.18$ in the control (Table~S2, note).

\Htwo\ fails in the form registered. Marginal coverage does fall from control
to break in 34 of the 50 admissible cells, but a fall is degradation only for
an arm that began below nominal, so the one-sided clause is unsupported: the
two-sided quantity actually tested, $|\text{coverage}-\text{nominal}|$
(Addendum~3), grows in only 27 of 50 (one cell, CNN-LC under split conformal,
rounds to 0.932 in both regimes and is counted unrounded). The break sorts the
grid by the side of nominal each arm began on---the sparse VAR natively 0.988
to 0.937 (on 297 valid cells), the NB head under a deep ensemble 0.988 to
0.956, against Poisson Lee--Carter 0.682 to 0.624 and APC/RH 0.731 to 0.654
(Table~S3). Without the control, those 34 cells read as uniform degradation.

\Hthree\ needs care. Joint path coverage lies below marginal coverage in all
100 (regime, cell) pairs of the two five-horizon regimes---mean gap 0.127 in
the control, 0.168 at the break, 0.362 over the placebo's nine
horizons---but independence alone would open $0.95-0.95^5=0.176$. Against each
arm's own implied independence, $\text{marginal}^H$, joint coverage
\emph{exceeds} the benchmark in 50 of 50 control, 50 of 50 shift and 45 of 46
placebo cells (the exception is the sparse VAR under the Poisson bootstrap), by
up to 0.344: horizons are strongly positively dependent, a dependence
\citet{dowd2010backtesting} name but do not measure. The registered comparator
of Addendum~3, each cell's model-implied joint coverage, is not computable from
the columns the sweeps emitted, and we report no verdict against it. Marginal
calibration does not buy the path: Poisson Lee--Carter falls from 0.624
marginal to 0.298 joint at the break. The copula band, the one mechanism
constructed over the path, holds 0.894--0.942 in the control and 0.805--0.927
at the break; over nine placebo horizons nothing holds it, the copula arm
included (0.544--0.939). Table~S4.

\Hfour\ is refuted as a universal and supported where the family-level split of
Section~\ref{sec:discussion-split} places it: the index extrapolators and the
LSTM. SVD Lee--Carter natively covers 0.888, 0.760 and 0.501 across ages
0--24, 25--64 and 65--99 in the control---four tenths of gradient owing nothing
to the pandemic. Calibrated arms are near-flat in age, and three reverse
outright at the break (65--99 against 25--64, margins under 0.05 discarded):
CBD natively and under the bootstrap, and neural-LC under dropout. CBD is
fitted on ages 55--99, so its 25--64 column is a 55--64 stub and is not
comparable with the full-age families. \citet{dowd2010backtesting} report
age-84 forecasts backtesting better than age-65, and Addendum~3 pre-committed
to treating such a reversal as informative rather than as grounds for
re-testing (Table~S5).

\Hfive\ is the severest result: calibration does not survive integration across
ages. In the control, SVD Lee--Carter natively covers 0.253 of its nominal
95\% intervals for $\annuity$ and 0.256 for $e_{65}$, and the LSTM deep
ensemble 0.157 for $\annuity$---an arm whose rate-level marginal coverage is
0.702 missing roughly three annuity intervals in four in a quiet period
(Table~S6). Integration concentrates the rate-surface failure rather than
averaging it away; reserving and longevity-hedging uses need that figure stated
in front of them.

Split conformal wrapping significantly improves the 95\% interval score of the
families whose native law was broken and worsens those whose native law was
sound: mean $\IS_{0.05}$ differences of $+0.694$ in the control and $+0.700$ at
the break for SVD Lee--Carter, against $-0.873$ and $-1.024$ for the NB head,
in five of the seven families that admit the contrast in the control (CBD
$p=0.198$ and APC/RH $p=0.086$ are indistinguishable from zero) and in all
seven at the break. Over the placebo's nine horizons the advantage is absent:
all six estimates favour the native law, SVD Lee--Carter at $-0.091$
(Table~S9). Wrap what is broken and do not wrap what is calibrated.

The remaining contribution is the protocol: a pre-registration hash-stamped
before the first fit, two validation gates---nominal coverage recovered on
synthetic truth, reference-implementation parity to machine precision---cluster
bootstrap inference that treats a pandemic as one event rather than thousands
of cells, and the matched control this study could not do without. Most of
these intervals were never calibrated to begin with; the break mainly re-orders
them, and the next one will not announce itself either.
