\begin{abstract}
Annuity and pension valuations price the \emph{spread} of a mortality
forecast, not only its centre: reserves, risk margins and longevity capital
all rest on the claim that a nominal 95\% prediction interval contains the
realised outcome about 95\% of the time. Stochastic mortality models have
produced such intervals since \citet{lee1992modeling}, and neural extensions
of Lee--Carter now produce them too, but whether any of these intervals hold
their nominal coverage when the mortality surface breaks has never been
tested on real data. The COVID-19 years supply the first sustained break in
modern single-age mortality data, and the June 2026 vintage of the Human
Mortality Database is the first with final annual data through 2024 for
twenty populations. We pre-register a calibration audit across that break.
Ten forecasting families (Lee--Carter, Poisson Lee--Carter, CBD, an
age--period--cohort model, a sparse VAR, a multi-output Gaussian process, and
four neural architectures) are crossed with seven uncertainty mechanisms
(model-native, Poisson bootstrap, deep ensembles, Monte Carlo dropout, split
conformal, EnbPI and a joint-path copula conformal band), giving fifty
admissible cells. Every cell emits predictive samples through one interface
and is scored by one code path: proper scores (CRPS, Poisson log score),
interval scores, marginal and joint-path coverage, randomised PIT, a
Murphy calibration--resolution decomposition, and the propagation of interval
error into life expectancy and annuity factors. Three regimes separate
ordinary forecast error from break-induced failure: a stable expanding-origin
control, the COVID break (train to 2019, test 2020--2024), and a placebo break
around 1914--1922. The crossed design lets a coverage failure be attributed to
the architecture or to the uncertainty machinery attached to it, a separation
no previous mortality study makes. The audit protocol---a synthetic-truth
validated harness, oracle parity with \pkg{StMoMo}, a pre-registration hash
committed before the first fit, and cluster-bootstrap forecast-comparison
inference that respects the single common shock---is itself the reusable
contribution. This draft is the pre-results skeleton: hypotheses, data,
design and metrics are fixed; all result tables are placeholders.
\end{abstract}
