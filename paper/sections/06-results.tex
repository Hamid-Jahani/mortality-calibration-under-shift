\noindent\textit{This section is a skeleton. Every table is a generated
fragment (\texttt{paper/tables/<name>.tex}, one \texttt{booktabs} body per
hook) produced by the analysis stage from the per-cell results files; every
figure is a generated \texttt{figures/<name>.pdf}. No number in this
section is typed by hand, and no fragment generated from a partial sweep
snapshot is quoted: such fragments carry a first-line comment marking them

regime, the number of populations, and---where a contrast is
involved---the number of cells dropped by the common-cell restriction of
Addendum~3 (\S11).}

\subsection{Validation gates}

Gates 1--3 (Section~\ref{sec:design-gates}) passed before any real-data
result was read. The one substantive finding from gate~1---the mild
over-dispersion of two-stage SVD Lee--Carter on Poisson data---is carried
into the reading of the SVD-LC rows below. A fourth, post-sweep gate
(\texttt{scripts/final\_qa.py}) classifies every cell that produced no
valid row before any table is generated; its output is the first table of
this section.

Table~\ref{tab:infeasible} lists every (population, family, mechanism) cell
of the primary grid that produced no valid row, in the three classes of the
QA gate. \emph{Structural} cells are design-floor cells: the panel is
shorter than the mechanism's calibration or ensemble-member requirement, or
falls under the registered admissibility floor of $n_{\text{train}}\ge 15$
complete training years (Addendum~3, \S4); the shortest shift panels
(training lengths in Table~\ref{tab:populations}) are of this kind, and
these cells are a property of the design, not a failure of any method.
\emph{Method} cells are those in which a family's own sampler declines to
produce a predictive law on that panel under a registered rule: the
explosive-coefficient-draw rejection of the sparse VAR (Addendum~3, \S7,
rejection and never clipping) and overflow of the Poisson composition on
bootstrap refits. The registered rejection rule does fire on real data for
the sparse VAR; which populations and arms it affects, and how many rows,
is reported in the table rather than here, and the finding is read as a
property of the family under its own rule. \emph{Machine} cells are
artefacts of a broken run and are re-run, never analysed around; the gate
refuses to pass while any exist. Error rows of every class are excluded
from every mean in this paper, and every contrast between arms below is
computed on the intersection of cells in which each compared arm produced
a valid row, with the intersection size reported (Addendum~3, \S11).
